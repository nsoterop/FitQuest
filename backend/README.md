Estimation references checked October 6:

- [Mifflin–St Jeor original study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2305711/): resting kcal/day = 10 × kg + 6.25 × cm − 5 × age, plus 5 for the reported male equation input or minus 161 for female. Profile pounds/inches convert with 0.45359237 and 2.54. The study population was 19–78; this implementation defers nutrition outside that range, leaving adult training available.
- [NIDDK Body Weight Planner](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/bwp): activity and individual inputs materially affect estimates; pregnancy/breastfeeding are outside its general scope. FitQuest uses a simpler starting estimate, **not** NIDDK's dynamic weight-change model. The coach selects an explicitly disclosed 1.4–2.2 activity factor from ordinary activity and the actual plan.
- [NIH ODS exercise nutrition guidance](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/ExerciseAndAthleticPerformance-HealthProfessional/): protein 1.2–2.0 g/kg and fat 20–35% of energy inform the permitted proposal ranges; carbohydrate supplies the remaining energy. No supplement recommendation is implemented.

Product guardrails are conservative automated-scope choices, not personalized medical thresholds: maintenance adjustment 0%; explicit loss −10–0%; gain 0–5%; at most 500 kcal estimated deficit; 1,500–5,000 kcal and never below estimated resting needs; height 120–230 cm, weight 35–250 kg, BMI at least 18.5. Outside these bounds or with reported special dietary needs, defer to individual guidance. Calories round to 50 kcal; grams round to whole numbers. Carbohydrate must remain at least 130 g and at most 10 g/kg; 4/4/9 macro energy must match within 5 kcal. Invalid/nonfinite/negative/intent-inconsistent proposals fail rather than silently clamp. Activity-factor and goal/training interpretations remain model judgments; clinical suitability is not independently verified. The visible result is a daily starting estimate with rationale/assumptions, not a precision or clearance claim.

## API

`GET /health` is public and returns exactly `{"service":"fitquest-api","status":"ok"}`. The service uses `PORT` (default 10000) and listens on `0.0.0.0`.

`GET /profile` and `POST /profile-intake` require a Bearer Supabase access token. Auth's `getUser(token)` verifies the exact token against this project's server. Anonymous/unconfirmed users and expired/mismatched identities are rejected; verified `sub` supplies ownership, never editable metadata or request ownership fields. Checking `auth.sessions` also rejects signed-out sessions whose access JWT has not expired yet. Auth or database outages fail closed. Both routes use `Cache-Control: no-store`; tokens, personal fields and parser errors are not logged or echoed.

`GET /profile` returns `{ "profile": null }` for a new account, otherwise the six fields below plus ISO `ageCapturedAt` and `updatedAt`. `POST /profile-intake` accepts these exact fields:

```json
{"firstName":"Alex","lastName":"Example","height":{"value":68,"unit":"in"},"age":29,"weight":{"value":155.5,"unit":"lb"},"gender":"Prefer not to say"}
```

Names/gender: 1–100 characters with non-whitespace text. Age: integer 1–130. Height: integer 1–120 inches. Weight: number greater than 0, up to 1500 lb. Extra properties, missing values, wrong units/types are rejected without coercion; body limit 4 KiB. Each owned UPSERT runs in a transaction and updates age-capture/update timestamps. Exactly `{"message":"User Data Saved"}` is returned only after successful COMMIT. Storage failures return 503.
