# Weekly exercise variety release October 12 2026

The owner assigned a generator correction, testing and publication while preserving saved plans. Newly generated weeks now explicitly request meaningful exercise variety while retaining similar training days, session goals, separate sessions, approximate durations and recovery balance. Suitable accessory, mobility and conditioning alternatives can rotate; useful core exercises can remain for skill and progression. Renaming an exercise or changing only its dose does not count as variety. Equipment, preferences, limitations and actual recorded work still govern suitability.

The guidance applies to new document weeks with progress context, including manual and scheduled generation. Initial plans and explicit saved-week reviews retain their existing instructions; review feedback governs revisions. Model, reasoning, schemas, consent, accounting, dispatch and persistence behavior are unchanged.
